## Rules for new pins

- 1000 × 1500 PNG made from `pin-template.html` in the Ages Unwrapped style. Check every render for anything cut off before posting.
- Draw everything with the shapes in `art.js`. Never use character art, brand logos or product photos.
- Write for grown-ups. No prices and no deal or discount claims (Amazon's rules). No safety or developmental claims beyond what the site's guides say. No delivery promises: say to check the delivery date on Amazon. No personal claims like "my kids love it" or "we tested it"; say why a pick is popular or useful instead.
- Only feature products that are on the site, and give the maker's age for each.
- Every description ends with "I earn from qualifying purchases."
- Don't repeat a topic that's already in the posted table unless the angle is clearly new.
